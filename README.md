# Thunderbolt Quantum Navigator UWP - Deprecated
This is the UWP counterpart of Thunderbolt Quantum Navigator for Windows 10/11 (Latest Releases). This application is still in beta and everything is subject to change. Thank you for testing our program

Development of Thunderbolt Quantum Navigator UWP is no more. Due to some unfortunate circumstances, we are saddened to share that updates meant to go towards Thunderbolt Quantum Navigator UWP have been permanently abandoned. Development efforts are going towards the new Thunderbolt Quantum Navigator - WinUI BETA, available at the Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PJF2RRGNM9P?hl=en-us&gl=GR&ocid=pdpshare
